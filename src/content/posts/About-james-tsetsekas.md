👋 Hi, I’m James Tsetsekas.

I’m a **Web & Digital Experience Developer at Great Plains Communications** and a **Product Engineer at [Conduit](https://conduit.market/)**. I build digital products at the intersection of software, commerce, infrastructure, and open protocols.

## What I work on

At Great Plains Communications, I work on public-facing web experiences and the systems behind them: performance, accessibility, content platforms, integrations, analytics, and the operational details that make a site dependable.

At Conduit, I help build decentralized commerce on Nostr and Bitcoin Lightning. That work spans product engineering, event-driven application architecture, signer integrations, encrypted order flows, payments, and the less glamorous reliability work needed to turn an open protocol into a product people can use.

The views on this blog are my own. When I write about Conduit, I discuss public architecture and open standards—not private company or customer information.

## What I write about

This blog is where I work through ideas in public. The recurring themes are:

- Bitcoin and Lightning operations
- Nostr, portable identity, and decentralized commerce
- Security engineering and incident analysis
- AI-assisted software development and agent safety
- Web performance, infrastructure, and product engineering
- Market structure and Bitcoin cycle research

I try to separate confirmed facts from interpretation, link primary sources, and update technical posts when the underlying guidance changes. Nothing here is financial advice.

## Projects and community

I maintain open-source and personal projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/JamesTsetsekas), including this blog and tools related to Bitcoin, data, and software infrastructure. I also organize the [Jersey City Bitcoin Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/jersey-city-bitcoin/) and enjoy helping people understand Bitcoin and the Lightning Network without requiring them to arrive as experts.

You can find a broader view of my work on my [portfolio](https://jamestsetsekas.com/) or reach me at [James@JamesTsetsekas.com](mailto:James@JamesTsetsekas.com).
