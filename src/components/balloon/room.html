How uncertain is this?

The tall footprint is an assumption, not a measurement. Try 160 ft² instead of 120 ft² and compare. Diameter changes act cubically; packing changes act linearly.

What is inside the boundary?

The whole-room model includes the living-room floor area and the double-height void. It excludes upstairs rooms, stairs and kitchen. Open connections at the stairs, upstairs and kitchen are artificially capped: a real pile would spill through unblocked openings.

Air-filled balloons settle on the floor and then pile up. No buoyancy, pressure, deformation, airflow or physical containment is simulated.
